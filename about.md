# Minimum

Modern general optimization mod for Geometry Dash.

Sequel to Algebra Dash by ConfiG / cgytrus. Built for Geode 5.x and GD 2.2081.

## What it does

Faster item pickup and counter updates
Skips empty sprite batch draw calls
Culls offscreen particle systems to cut lag spikes
Fast alt tab without waiting on saves
Optional force RGBA8888 textures
Optional MSAA anti aliasing

## Dense levels

On levels with thousands of objects the biggest gains come from Fast Pickup, Skip Empty Batches, and Cull Offscreen Particles. Keep those on.
